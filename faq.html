Questions

A few things guests often ask. If yours is not here, we are always happy to talk.

How do I arrange a stay?

Send us a note with your dates, and we will personally confirm availability, rates, and next steps.

Can I return each season?

We would love that. Returning guests hold a special place here, and we are glad to keep the same dates for you next year. Stays are seasonal only, with a preference for guests who return year after year.

Where is the house?

The house is in Schroon Lake, NY, in the Adirondacks. See Location & Area.

How many people does it sleep?

Sleeps 8.

Is the house available year-round?

The house is offered by the season, and our family spends part of each year there. See Rates & Availability for open and blocked periods.

Is the house listed on Airbnb or VRBO?

No. Stays are arranged personally with our family.
